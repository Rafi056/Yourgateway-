# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Institution listing and institution detail pages
- Public announcements page
- Public legal/about pages
- Shared crawlability assets (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, HTML shell metadata)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- Admin pages (`/admin`)
- API-only endpoints except where they affect public crawlability or metadata

## Target audience
- Arabic-speaking students and families evaluating study opportunities in Malaysia
- Secondary English-speaking audience researching Malaysian universities and language centers

- جامعات ماليزيا
- معاهد اللغة في ماليزيا
